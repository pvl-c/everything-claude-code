# Service blueprint: Acquisition Studio

## Публичное обещание

> We turn a cross-border property or hospitality opportunity into an evidence-based acquisition decision and a controlled closing process.

Не «мы найдём лучший объект» и не «AI рассчитает доходность».

## Этапы и definition of done

### Gate 0 — Fit

**Вход:** intro/referral/content lead.  
**Done:** подтверждены asset intent, minimum equity, geography, timing, decision-makers и готовность к KYC/paid work.  
**Отказ:** нет капитала, ожидание гарантированного ВНЖ/доходности, отказ от KYC, дешёвая аренда, неопределённый «покажите варианты».

### Gate 1 — Thesis

**Вход:** оплаченный H01.  
**Done:** клиент утвердил buy box, role (operator/passive), total capital at risk, exclusions и stop criteria.  
**Deliverable:** Investment Thesis v1.

### Gate 2 — Mandate

**Вход:** scope/fee/conflicts подписаны, retainer получен.  
**Done:** RACI партнёров, sourcing channels, communication cadence и data rules утверждены.

### Gate 3 — Shortlist

**Вход:** минимум данных объекта.  
**Done:** не больше 3–5 объектов с сравнимыми полями; «unknown» отображается как unknown, а не ноль.

### Gate 4 — Deal Screen

**Вход:** data pack и permission.  
**Done:** H03 с base/downside/upside, red flags, specialists and cost-to-validate. Это ещё не formal due diligence.

### Gate 5 — Commitment

**Вход:** LOI/arras strategy reviewed.  
**Done:** conditions, deposits, timelines and exit/termination rights рассмотрены соответствующими advisers; client decision logged.

### Gate 6 — Due Diligence

**Вход:** full data room.  
**Done:** каждый workstream подписан named professional; unresolved risks приняты/mitigated/rejected клиентом.

### Gate 7 — Closing

**Done:** final funds/costs/titles/conditions проверены ответственными сторонами; client package архивирован.

### Gate 8 — Launch handoff

**Done:** owner, operator, insurer, accountant and key vendors назначены; baseline KPIs и first-100-day risks зафиксированы.

## Партнёрская сеть и RACI

| Workstream | Accountable | Responsible specialist | AI role |
|---|---|---|---|
| relationship/mandate | основательница | deal coordinator | draft/admin |
| property/legal | client-appointed lawyer | lawyer | extract/compare only |
| planning/licence | lawyer/architect as agreed | specialist | checklist/gap only |
| technical/capex | technical lead | architect/engineer | organise evidence |
| business/financial | M&A/finance lead | accountant/adviser | normalize draft/flags |
| tax/structure | licensed tax adviser | adviser | no conclusion |
| AML/KYC | obligated entity/compliance owner | KYC provider | workflow/status only |
| software/data | tech partner | tech partner | implementation/monitoring |

## Communication

- Portal — source of truth.
- WhatsApp — notification and relationship, not document archive.
- Weekly one-page decision brief.
- No more than one client decision request per issue; alternatives and consequence shown.
- Emergency escalation channel defined at mandate start.
